Stump Grinding & Removal in Peoria, IL

Eliminate that unsightly stump for good. Fast, affordable stump grinding throughout Peoria and Central Illinois.

That stump in your yard isn't just an eyesore — it's a trip hazard, a pest magnet, and it's stopping you from using your yard the way you want. Peoria Tree Service uses commercial-grade grinding equipment to remove stumps completely, leaving you with a clean, usable yard.

Why Remove That Stump?

  • Safety hazard — Stumps cause trips and falls, especially for kids and elderly family members
  • Pest attraction — Decaying stumps attract termites, carpenter ants, beetles, and other wood-boring insects that can spread to your home
  • Disease spread — A diseased stump can harbor fungal infections that spread to healthy nearby trees
  • Lawn care obstacles — Mowing around stumps is frustrating and damages mower blades
  • Property appearance — A clean yard significantly improves curb appeal and property value
  • New planting or construction — Clear the space for a new tree, garden bed, patio, or structure

Our Stump Grinding Process

We use professional stump grinding machines to reduce stumps below ground level — typically 8–12 inches deep:

  • We assess the stump size, location, and any underground utilities before starting
  • Our grinder is maneuvered into position and the stump is ground down in sections
  • The resulting wood chips and mulch can be left on-site for use in garden beds, or hauled away
  • We fill the hole with the wood chip mulch and rake the area clean
  • You can plant grass seed or sod within a few weeks once the mulch settles

Stump Grinding vs. Stump Removal

Stump grinding (what we do) uses a mechanical grinder to reduce the stump to wood chips below the soil surface. The roots are left in the ground and decompose naturally over time. This is the most efficient and affordable option for nearly all residential situations.

Full stump removal involves excavating the entire root system — a much more labor-intensive process usually only necessary for construction or replanting in the same exact spot. We can discuss this option during your estimate if needed.
