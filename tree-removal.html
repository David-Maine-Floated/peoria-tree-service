When Should a Tree Be Removed?

Not every struggling tree needs to come down, but some situations make removal the right call:

  • Dead or dying trees — Structurally weak and a serious hazard in wind or ice storms
  • Storm-damaged trees — Split trunks, major broken limbs, or uprooted root systems
  • Disease or pest infestation — Emerald ash borer, oak wilt, and other issues that can't be reversed
  • Trees too close to structures — Roots damaging foundations, branches threatening your roof or power lines
  • Overcrowding — Removing one tree can save the others around it
  • New construction or landscaping — Clearing for a deck, garage, driveway, or pool

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Peoria?

In most cases, no permit is required for removing a tree on private property in Peoria, IL. Exceptions may apply to certain protected species or trees in specific zones. We can help advise during your estimate.

Do you haul away the wood and debris?

Yes — complete cleanup and debris removal is included in every tree removal job. We chip branches on-site and haul away all material. You can also request we leave wood sections for firewood at no extra charge.

How quickly can you schedule a tree removal?

Non-emergency removals are typically scheduled within 3–7 business days. Emergency situations are given priority and we often respond the same day.
